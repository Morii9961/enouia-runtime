# B2.1 exact-pending retry record

Date: 2026-09-28. Scope: record one sanitized failed delivery disposition in the hashed `delivery.json` of a new immutable generation under the Activity writer lock. The record contains the pending sequence and exact byte hash, failure count, stable error code, next eligible time, and optional last transport time. It has no raw subprocess output or credentials.

Generation validation rejects a retry record without pending, with a mismatched sequence/hash, zero failure count, malformed error code, or invalid time relationship. The writer increments the count without changing archive, high-water sequence, or pending bytes; an attempted transport updates its time, while a later observation-only failure retains it. The caller chooses the next eligible instant, bounded from now to 24 hours ahead. On a matching public acknowledgment, the new generation removes the retry record while preserving historical generations.

Synthetic Windows tests cover two successive failure commits, persistence across selected generations, unchanged exact pending bytes, rejection without pending or with an excessive delay, invalid imported retry records, and clearing after public acknowledgment. Automatic eligibility and manual override are separate run-decision work; this feature alone does not schedule or send a retry. It does not establish production delivery or power-loss durability.
